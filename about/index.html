About

About JEEVisionX

Visual lessons for JEE Main and Advanced, built on one belief: a concept you can see is a concept you keep.

JEEVisionX is an initiative by an alumnus of the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) and Netaji Subhas University of Technology (NSUT), with more than twenty years of professional experience in academia, research and industry.

Over those years, one pattern kept showing up. A student could recite a formula and still not know what it meant. Another page of algebra rarely fixed that. A good picture usually did, often in a few seconds.

Our vision

We want every JEE concept to be something you can see. When a complex number visibly turns as you multiply it, or a wheel rolls and you watch its lowest point stand still for an instant, the idea stops being a rule to memorise. It becomes something you understand, and can use on a question you have never seen before.

Learning gets lighter when you can see what is happening. And the understanding goes deeper, because you are not holding on to a formula; you know where it came from.

What JEEVisionX is

JEEVisionX makes animated lessons for JEE Main and JEE Advanced. Each lesson takes one idea and builds it on screen, one step at a time. The algebra is shown beside the picture, so you see both sides of the same fact. Every lesson ends on a solved problem, so you finish able to do something, not just having watched something.

We are starting with Mathematics, chapter by chapter. Physics and Chemistry will follow.

Every number, graph and claim in a lesson is checked by an independent calculation before it is published. JEE students notice mistakes, and they should never have to wonder whether the lesson is right.

Each lesson also has free notes on this website: the idea in short, the formulas, a solved example, the common trap, and a practice set with answers. You do not need to sign up or pay anything. Short videos on YouTube and Instagram carry one idea each, in under a minute.

Who it is for

  • Students preparing for JEE Main and Advanced, in Class 11, in Class 12, or taking a drop year.
  • Class 11 and 12 students studying the same chapters for their board exams.
  • Anyone who knows a formula but not why it works, and wants to see the reason.
  • Teachers and tutors who want a clear picture to show in class. You are welcome to use the lessons and share the notes with your students.

Join us

New lessons come out regularly. Follow along wherever you prefer, and write to us with questions, corrections, or the topic you want to see next.
