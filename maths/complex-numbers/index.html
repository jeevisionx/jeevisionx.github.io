Lesson 1

Modulus and Argument of a Complex Number

A complex number is a point, and also an arrow from the origin. Its modulus is how long the arrow is. Its argument is which way the arrow points.

Lesson 2

Multiplication of Complex Numbers as Rotation

Multiplying by a complex number w turns the arrow by arg w and scales its length by |w|. The lengths multiply and the angles add, so one multiplication can rotate any point about the origin.
